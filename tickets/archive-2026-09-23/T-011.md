# T-011 — Restore WCAG AA 3:1 contrast on search and journey input borders

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md DES-01 | Severity: P1 | Class: BROKEN  
**Assigned tier:** INTERMEDIATE  
**Effort estimate:** 1.5h | **Sequence:** after T-008; before T-014

## WHAT
Restore WCAG 2.1 AA compliant non-text contrast (minimum 3:1) on the default input borders of the editorial redesign. Introduce `--tsc-line-strong` and its Tailwind alias in `app/globals.css`. Use a value such as `#6d6b63`, which is 5.34:1 against white and 4.73:1 against `--tsc-paper` (`#f4f1e9`). Do not use `#848CA0`: it is 2.98:1 against paper and therefore misses the stated threshold.

## WHY
Ticket T-007 previously corrected input border contrast on the legacy contact and booking forms to `#848CA0` (3.36:1 contrast against white). However, the recently added editorial journey components (`ProblemInput.tsx`) and catalog search (`SearchBar.tsx`) reverted to using `--tsc-line` (`#d7d2c7`), which measures only 1.35:1 against paper and 1.52:1 against white, failing WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 1.4.11 (Non-text Contrast) and reducing usability for low-vision users.
