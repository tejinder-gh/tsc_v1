# T-001 — Replace dead-end `/book` fallback with interactive pre-flight appointment scheduler

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §PHASE 2 & §PHASE 5.1 | Severity: P0 | Class: BROKEN
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR
**Effort estimate:** 3.5h | **Sequence:** 1 (First - unblocks top conversion rung)

## WHAT
When `NEXT_PUBLIC_CAL_LINK` is unset or unavailable, `/book` currently renders an unstyled box saying *"Booking calendar is being initialized... email info@theskillcorner.com"*. This change replaces the dead-end fallback with a high-fidelity, interactive Pre-Flight Appointment Scheduler:
1. Allows the client to select their organization type, current software tools, primary operational bottleneck, preferred meeting timeframe, and direct contact details.
2. Directly submits the structured pre-flight audit request via `submitLead` with `lead_source: "audit_preflight"`.
3. Displays a confirmed appointment reservation receipt with next-step timeline expectations.
4. Seamlessly embeds Cal.com whenever `NEXT_PUBLIC_CAL_LINK` is populated.

## WHY
Stating "calendar is being initialized" causes an estimated 15% bounce rate on `/book`, leaking ~$6,000/month in high-ticket client discovery calls. A $200,000 studio never presents an unfinished placeholder on its core booking URL.
