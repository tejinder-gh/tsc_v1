# T-005 — Show a payback-period figure in the ROI calculator for the practice segment

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md CFO §5B | Severity: P1 | Class: MISSING
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** independent

## WHAT
When a visitor's segment is `"practice"`, add a line to the ROI calculator's result panel (`components/home/RoiCalculator.tsx`) that translates the visitor's own slider-driven annual savings figure into an approximate **projected** payback period against the practice engagement's published build-fee range, e.g. "Projected payback: roughly X–Y months, based on the numbers above and our typical $7,500–$25,000 engagement range — confirmed exactly after your free audit." The local segment (and the "unknown"/no-segment case) is unaffected — this is practice-segment-only.

**Pricing figures are NOT changing in this ticket.** The user reviewed the $7,500–$25,000 build / $1,500/month support figures against live 2026 market data for bespoke multi-workflow AI automation agency engagements (project fees commonly $15,000–$50,000 for multi-workflow builds, retainers $1,000–$7,000/month for small-to-mid business per multiple industry sources) and confirmed the existing figures are realistic — if anything conservative — for this category, and should be kept as-is. What changes here is framing only: this is a genuinely new type of claim (an implied timeframe-to-value), and the user asked explicitly for "projected"/"anticipated" wording rather than anything read as a guarantee, to avoid the calculator's numeric confidence bleeding into a claim about a specific dollar figure the site hasn't actually committed to for any individual visitor.

## WHY
Live competitor research (AUDIT_REPORT.md CMO §4A) found turnkey SaaS alternatives (Enamly AI, PatientXpress, CallBird AI, etc.) priced at $299–$1,500/month with an overlapping surface-level pitch, versus this site's practice tier at $7,500–$25,000 up front plus $1,500/month support. The site's own ROI calculator already computes a personal annual-savings figure from the visitor's inputs — this ticket closes the gap between "here's what manual work costs you" and "here's why the higher build fee is still the better trade," using numbers the visitor already entered rather than a new claim. This directly targets the price objection identified in the audit at the moment it would otherwise cause drop-off. The "projected" framing constraint mirrors the site's existing discipline for illustrative numbers (see `content/proof.ts`'s "Example –" / "Anticipated outcome" labeling, applied there for the same reason: numbers derived from a formula or category, not a specific measured/promised outcome, must read as such).

`lib/roi.ts` should hold any new payback-period math, alongside the existing `annualCost`/`monthlyCost` pure functions — keep math out of the component, matching the existing separation of concerns. If you add a function there, add matching tests to `lib/roi.test.ts`.
