# T-013 — Add blueprint capture form to journey demonstration completion

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md CPO-01 / CFO | Severity: P1 | Class: MISSING  
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR  
**Effort estimate:** 3h | **Sequence:** after T-010

## WHAT
Add a high-converting "Email Me This Blueprint" action to the completion state of the interactive demonstration in `DemonstrationView.tsx`. When a visitor completes or interacts with a demonstration scenario (System Spec, Decision Router, Pipeline Stream, or Educational Blueprint), provide an accessible modal or inline form (`BlueprintCaptureModal.tsx`) allowing them to enter their name, email, and business name. Submitting this form calls `POST /api/lead` with `lead_source: "demonstration_blueprint"`, bundling the active scenario title, opportunity key, and recommended architecture into the lead submission metadata.

## WHY
The CPO and CFO audit revealed that users who invest several minutes exploring the interactive demonstration on the homepage currently face an all-or-nothing choice: book a 30-minute call or abandon the page. Conservative estimates show ~20% of demonstration viewers want to review or share the specification with their business partners before taking a sales call. Capturing these leads prevents an estimated $3,600–$5,400/month in high-intent pipeline decay.
