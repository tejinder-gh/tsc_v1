# T-017 — Retire the unbound team invitation surface

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §F-02 | Severity: P1 | Class: BROKEN
**Assigned tier:** SENIOR
**Effort estimate:** 3.0 hours | **Sequence:** after T-016

## WHAT

Remove the dashboard Team & Invites page, the public invitation-redemption route, and the file-backed team/invitation repository. The product must no longer display or generate invitation links, roles, or access-management claims that do not create Clerk access or affect authorization. Keep the dashboard as a single-operator surface authorized by T-016.

## WHY

The current feature stores plaintext bearer tokens and personal emails in ignored local JSON, but redeeming a token only creates an application record. It does not create a Clerk session, membership, role, or permission. Leaving this UI online is a direct trust and security misrepresentation.
