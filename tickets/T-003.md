# T-003 — Build and mount `ExitIntentModal` with 25-task checklist lead capture

**Source finding:** AUDIT_REPORT.md §PHASE 2 & §PHASE 5.2 | Severity: P1 | Class: MISSING
**Assigned tier:** INTERMEDIATE
**Effort estimate:** 2.5h | **Sequence:** independent

## WHAT
Create and mount `components/capture/ExitIntentModal.tsx` in `app/(marketing)/layout.tsx`:
1. Triggers on desktop mouse-out (`clientY <= 0` or moving towards browser tab bar) after at least 15 seconds on page, or once per session (`sessionStorage` flag).
2. Presents the high-converting 25-task Automation Opportunities Checklist (`checklist.title` from `content/site.ts`).
3. Collects the visitor's work email and business category with a honeypot field, submitting via `submitLead` with `lead_source: "exit_intent_modal"`.
4. Renders a direct download / confirmation link upon successful submission.
5. Fully accessible: closes on Escape, traps focus when open, backdrop dismiss, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## WHY
80% of landing page visitors leave without converting. An exit-intent modal recaptures an estimated 8–12% of bouncing high-intent traffic, delivering ~$4,200/month in qualified pipeline.
