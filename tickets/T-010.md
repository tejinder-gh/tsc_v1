## WHAT
Build and mount `components/roi/RoiCalculator.tsx` on `/results` (and as an embeddable section):
1. **Interactive Inputs:**
   - Team Size (1 to 50 employees / staff)
   - Average Hourly Loaded Wage ($25 to $150/hr)
   - Repetitive Tasks Handled (slider: hours per week spent on manual intake, booking, follow-up: 5 to 40 hrs/wk)
2. **Dynamic Mathematical Engine (`lib/roi.ts`):**
   - Annual Hours Recaptured = `weeklyHours * 50 weeks`
   - Annual Wage Savings = `Annual Hours * hourlyWage`
   - Estimated System Deployment Cost = scaled dynamically by team size / complexity
   - Payback Period = calculated in days/months
   - 3-Year Net ROI Multiple = `((3 * Annual Savings) - Deployment Cost) / Deployment Cost`
3. **Conversion Bridge:**
   - Directly links calculation results to `/book` with pre-filled scope context or lets visitors copy their customized ROI summary.
4. **Visual Aesthetics:** High-end editorial financial card styling with tabular-mono numbers, range sliders matching `--tsc-action`, and WCAG AA contrast.

## WHY
High-ticket enterprise buyers ($50,000–$200,000 contracts) require clear financial justification before booking. Giving prospects a tactile, deterministic calculator directly proves economic payback and unlocks an estimated $18,750/month in qualified pipeline.
