## 1. The Real Cost of a Missed Call

Before looking at what you spend, you must understand what you’re losing. 

Many software vendors claim that missed calls cost small businesses upwards of $120,000 a year. Let's look at a realistic, bottom-up calculation for a typical GTA local business or clinic:

* **Inquiry Volume:** 30 calls or web inquiries per week.
* **Unanswered Rate:** 25% (missed during rushes, after hours, or when staff are with other clients) = **7.5 missed inquiries per week.**
* **Hang-Up Rate:** 70% of missed callers who reach voicemail hang up without leaving a message and call a competitor = **5.25 lost opportunities per week.**
* **Conversion Rate:** Assume 30% of inquiries normally convert to a booking/job = **1.57 lost clients per week.**
* **Average Job/Lifetime Value:** Assume $150 (typical for trade calls, clinical sessions, or salon treatments).

$$\text{Weekly Revenue Leak} = 1.57 \text{ lost bookings} \times \$150 = \$235.50 \text{ per week}$$
$$\text{Annual Revenue Leak} = \$235.50 \text{ per week} \times 52 \text{ weeks} = \$12,246.00 \text{ per year}$$

For a business taking just 30 calls a week, the cost of unanswered phones is **over $1,000 a month** in lost revenue. For professional practices with higher transaction values (like law firms or dental clinics where a client lifetime value exceeds $2,000), a single missed inquiry a week can represent a **$10,000+ monthly leak.**

## 2. AI Answering Options: The Three Main Paths

There are three ways to get an AI receptionist running in your business. Here is a breakdown of their pricing structures:

### Path A: The DIY AI SaaS Tool (Raw Software)
* **What it is:** Software platforms where you sign up, input credit card details, write your own instructions, and configure the phone routing yourself.
* **Examples & Pricing:**
  * **Smith.ai (AI Plan):** Starts around $97.50/month (base charge + per-chat/call fees). Hybrid human plans range from $95 to $300+.
  * **Goodcall:** Starts at $59 to $99/month.
  * **Slang.ai:** ~$399/month (focused heavily on restaurants).
  * **Synthflow / VAPI:** Developer-first voice tools charging fractions of a cent per minute (typically $0.05–$0.15/minute), but require intermediate coding skills to set up.

### Path B: The Proprietary Software Suite (Annual Contract)
* **What it is:** Complete customer communication and reputation suites that include AI messaging tools as an add-on.
* **Examples & Pricing:**
  * **Podium:** $399 to $599/month/location.
  * **Birdeye:** $299 to $449/month/location.
  * **The Catch:** Both platforms require **12-month auto-renewing contracts.** If you decide it's not working after two months, you are still liable for thousands of dollars.
