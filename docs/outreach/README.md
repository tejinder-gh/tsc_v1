# Outreach Campaigns & Personas

This directory contains human-written, highly citable, peer-to-peer cold emails for **The Skill Corner**'s main target audiences. 

The emails are designed to sound like a smart colleague sharing a relevant observation, avoiding standard "salesy" marketing jargon, and keeping friction extremely low.

## Best Practices for Sending

### 1. Subject Lines
- Keep them **2-4 words, lowercase, with no punctuation**.
- Make them look like internal emails a colleague would write (e.g. `no-shows`, `missed calls`, `intake admin`).
- **Never** use fake "Re:" or emojis.

### 2. Contractions & Tone
- Use contractions (`don't`, `it's`, `we're`) to maintain a natural, conversational flow.
- Lead with **their world**, not yours. (Avoid: "My name is X and I work at Y...").

### 3. Interest-Based CTAs
- Instead of asking for a 30-minute meeting, use low-friction asks:
  - *"Worth exploring?"*
  - *"Would this be useful?"*
  - *"Should I send over a quick video of how it works?"*

### 4. Cadence & Follow-Ups
- Space emails by **3 to 5 days** initially, expanding to **7+ days** for later touches.
- Every follow-up must offer a **new angle** (e.g., a new metric, case study, or resource like `/checklist`).
