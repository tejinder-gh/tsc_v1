# The Automation Opportunities Checklist

**25 tasks your business can stop doing by hand**

Print this or keep it open, tick every task you (or your office manager) still do
manually, fill in the score column, and you'll know exactly which automation pays
you back first. No jargon - every item says what gets automated and roughly how
many hours a week it gives back.

**How to score:** for each ticked item, multiply the hours it eats per week by how
much you dread it (1 = mildly annoying, 2 = draining, 3 = the reason Sunday nights
feel bad). Highest score = automate first.
